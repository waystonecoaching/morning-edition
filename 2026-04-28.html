Neurophysiological and autonomic patterns in heartfulness and bell meditation

Three-arm study on EEG and autonomic markers across heartfulness and bell-based meditative forms. The contemplative tradition you stand in is not foreign to this map. Stillness has a physiology, and the autonomic substrate is the language Porges and Dana taught the field to read. Read it as a brother of the Fathers might, with curiosity rather than appetite.

Randomized three-arm exploratory study examining neural oscillations and autonomic markers (heart rate variability, respiration) across heart-centered meditation, sound-based bell meditation, and a control. Reports differential patterns in brain-body coupling between forms, suggesting these are not interchangeable practices at the autonomic level.

Source: Frontiers in Psychology
