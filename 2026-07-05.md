**Why this matters to you:** The VA is now running its own MDMA-assisted therapy trial rather than waiting on outside sponsors, which is the institutional shift that matters more than any single result. Note the design choices: treatment-resistant PTSD with co-occurring alcohol use disorder, both required, randomized against an active placebo with structured therapy. That is a harder and more honest test than the MAPS phase 3 designs that drew FDA criticism. Clinical territory throughout; the coaching-relevant part is what members will be reading and asking about.

**The story:** The VA began enrolling veterans in May in its first-ever clinical trial of MDMA-assisted therapy, targeting treatment-resistant PTSD with co-occurring alcohol use disorder. Roughly 80 veterans will be randomized to MDMA-assisted therapy or an identical therapy structure with an active placebo, led from the Providence VA Medical Center with a second site in Connecticut. It sits inside a wider VA psychedelic research push of 19 active studies, with results not expected until around 2030.

**Actionable:** Note the 2030 results date. When a client cites this trial as a reason to seek MDMA now, that timeline is the grounding fact.
