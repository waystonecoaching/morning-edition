Eight weeks of guided slow breathing alters stress responses in veterans - PsyPost

Eight weeks of slow breathing measurably shifted stress physiology in veterans. This is the nervous-system substrate you already build on, tested in exactly your population. It gives you a defensible, non-clinical intervention to hand a client between sessions.

A study found that eight weeks of guided slow breathing altered stress responses in veterans, shifting autonomic markers toward regulation. The effect showed up in physiological measures, not just self-report. It adds evidence to the claim that paced breathing is a trainable route into ventral-vagal regulation.

Try this: Offer a client one paced-breathing protocol, six breaths a minute, and track over eight weeks whether their own felt sense of state shifts. Keep it framed as regulation practice, not treatment.
