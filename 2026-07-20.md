**The story:** The paper argues moral injury is conceptually distinct from PTSD and examines how the DSM-5-TR moral problems Z-code could support that separation in assessment and documentation. **Actionable:** Revisit how you screen for moral injury versus trauma at intake. A betrayal-and-leadership-failure story is not a fear-conditioning story, and the coaching arc differs.
