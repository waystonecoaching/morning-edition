**The story:** An NIH-funded randomized trial tested Traditions and Connections for Urban Native Americans, TACUNA, a virtual program blending evidence-based prevention with traditional cultural practice. **The story:** The article defines moral injury as social, psychological, and spiritual harm arising from betrayal of core values such as justice, fairness, and loyalty. Recovery, the author argues, starts with listening and acknowledgment, and many find that having suffering heard and validated is itself a decisive step.
