**Why this matters to you:** Subthreshold PTSD is most of what walks into a coaching practice. **The story:** This Journal of Traumatic Stress study combines analytic frameworks to weight PTSD symptoms rather than count them equally, and applies the result to both full-threshold and subthreshold classification.
