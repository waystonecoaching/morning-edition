**Why this matters to you:** This is the first multisite look at what happens when psilocybin leaves the trial and enters a regulated commercial market. It matters because it is the closest thing the field has to a picture of what your clients would actually walk into if Arizona ever moved. Read it as observational data, not efficacy proof. There is no control arm, participants self-selected, and the follow-up is three months. Hold the safety signal and the outcome signal separately.

**The story:** OHSU researchers followed 346 people through single psilocybin sessions at 24 licensed Oregon service centers between November 2024 and June 2026, publishing in JAMA Network Open. At three months participants reported drops in moderate-to-severe depression, anxiety, and PTSD symptoms. Four first-time users had adverse behavioral reactions requiring medical attention, no serious physical events were reported, and seven people described the experience as harmful at three months.

**Actionable:** If a client raises Oregon services, this is the study to hand them, along with the caveat that self-selected observational data is not the same as a treatment recommendation. Referral to a licensed facilitator is a clinical-adjacent decision and sits outside a coaching scope.

This paper works on weighting symptoms rather than counting them, which is a step toward describing that person accurately. Useful for how you think, not for anything you would say diagnostically.

**The story:** Researchers combined analytic frameworks to incorporate symptom weighting into the classification of full-threshold and subthreshold PTSD. **Actionable:** Use it as a reminder that impairment and diagnosis are different variables. Assessment and diagnosis remain outside coaching scope; what changes is your language about why someone who does not qualify for treatment can still be struggling badly.

**Actionable:** Read the construct definition, not the score. The way MORIS separates event exposure from distress is a clean structure for how you frame moral injury with a client without reaching for a number.
