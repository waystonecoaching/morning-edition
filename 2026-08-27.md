## Editor's Note

Two numbers set today's frame, and they are the same number. Nearly two thirds of Texas first responders carry a morally injurious event. Nearly two thirds of trauma survivors regain their footing without any intervention at all. Both are true, and holding them together is the whole discipline: take the wound seriously without declaring it permanent. The rest of the issue is what sits between those two figures, in law, in policy, in training, and in the quiet decision a man makes to call someone after a bad call.
