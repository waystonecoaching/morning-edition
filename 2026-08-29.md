Two studies today do the same quiet work from opposite ends. Oregon's psilocybin data says the ground is more stable than the field's critics feared. **Why this matters to you:** This is the first multisite look at a legal, non-clinical psilocybin market, and it is the strongest real-world safety signal the field has produced. It matters because the credibility of psychedelic research has been under strain since the Lykos rejection, and naturalistic outcome data from a regulated market answers a different question than a Phase 3 trial does. Read it as evidence about the delivery model, not about efficacy against a control. The PTSD signal is a scope-of-practice edge: these are facilitated sessions, not therapy, and the distinction is exactly the one integration coaching lives inside.

**The story:** Researchers followed 346 adults who took a single psilocybin session with licensed facilitators at 24 Oregon service centers between November 2024 and June 2026, surveying them at baseline, one week, one month, and three months. The study is observational with no control group, so it measures what happened in the market, not what psilocybin causes.
