## Editor's Note

Two lineages sit next to each other at the top of this issue and they do not agree. One says the responder is injured in the body and can be restored. The other says the wound is to conscience, and no protocol reaches it. Hold both without resolving them. The tension is the honest place to stand, and a brother in that tension is better served by company than by an answer.
