**The story:** This case study analyzed 517 poems across nine Gary Snyder collections spanning 1959 to 2015, using computational text analysis measuring emotion vocabulary, self-reference, and sensory language, with poet Wendell Berry as a non-practicing comparison to isolate practice effects from ordinary aging.
