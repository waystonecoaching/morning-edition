Davis, Farchione and Colleagues Present FDA's Psychedelic Drug Framework in NEJM

The regulator is putting its reasoning in print. It sets the terms every MDMA and psilocybin program for responders gets judged by, including the credibility questions the field has not shaken since the MDMA rejection. Read the NEJM piece itself, not the summary.

FDA staff published a commentary in the New England Journal of Medicine laying out what they call a new framework for psychedelic drugs. It refines the 2023 draft guidance after roughly 200 public comments and lessons from live development programs. The agency stresses urgency around depression and PTSD, points to Breakthrough designations and rolling and priority review, and says psychedelic therapies remain investigational and the approach is still evolving. The news summary does not carry the trial design detail, so functional unblinding and the therapy component are unverified here.

Assessing the feasibility and acceptability of training first responder peer supporters in trauma risk management (TRiM)

The edge between peer and clinical is where these programs get in trouble.

A Journal of Traumatic Stress early-view paper assesses whether first responder peer supporters can be trained in Trauma Risk Management, and whether the training is acceptable to them.
