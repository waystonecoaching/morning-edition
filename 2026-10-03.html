No. 01 · Psychedelic Therapy · 2026-08-10

MAPS Responds to Report of Progress for MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD with FDA

The biggest MDMA story in months and the one to read coldly. Resilient Pharmaceuticals, the company that was MAPS PBC and then Lykos, has resubmitted its NDA after the August 2024 Complete Response Letter. The source is MAPS itself, so this is advocacy press, not a regulator speaking. Credibility stress still sits on this field: the CRL was about trial conduct and unblinding, and a resubmission is not an approval. Scope edge: any first responder PTSD referral toward MDMA work stays clinical, not coaching.

MAPS says Resilient Pharmaceuticals resubmitted its NDA for MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD on August 9, 2026, with no additional Phase 3 trial. MAPS says it has no active role in the program now. Rick Doblin reads the resubmission as 'not yet' rather than 'no' from FDA, and credits the new FDA psychedelic guidance with making resubmission possible. Nothing in the statement settles the 2024 objections on its own. FDA has not acted.

Try this: Watch for the FDA acknowledgment and review clock. Until then, treat MDMA for responder PTSD as investigational when clients ask.
Source: MAPS
