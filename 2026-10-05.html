Feasibility and acceptability of training first responder peer supporters in trauma risk management (TRiM)

TRiM is a structured peer model, and this is a feasibility study on training responders to deliver it. That is the evidence base peer support programs get asked for. Peer support sits next to, not inside, clinical care. Hold that line when you describe your own role.

Journal of Traumatic Stress published an early-view study on whether first responder peer supporters can be trained in TRiM and whether the approach is acceptable to them. Feasibility and acceptability are early-stage outcomes, not proof of symptom change.

Try this: Check whether Phoenix Fire's peer team uses TRiM or something close, and what outcome data exists.
