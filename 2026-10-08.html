FDA Floats Ibogaine Trial Design as Psychedelic Comment Period Closes

Regulatory signal, not a treatment story. FDA issued an ibogaine RFI the same day its psychedelic considerations comment period closed. Ibogaine sits on the high-acuity edge: cardiac risk, medication washouts, opioid use disorder populations. Well outside coaching scope, but it shows how cautious the agency is being as the field presses for speed. Trade newsletter, so read it as a pointer to the primary FDA notice.

FDA published a request for information on ibogaine clinical studies, sketching a hypothetical early-stage design it says fits its July final guidance. Dosing is ascending and sequential, capped at 10 mg/kg, one participant at a time, with escalation gated by a safety committee, a monitoring board, and FDA. Dosing is inpatient with continuous cardiac monitoring for up to 36 hours, optional CYP2D6 genotyping, washout of opioid agonists, antipsychotics, antiemetics, and SSRIs, and 12 months of cognitive testing. HHS is coordinating the work across NIDA and ARPA-H. The same bulletin notes Compass talking pricing and a slow launch, and New Mexico's medical psilocybin program starting up.

Try this: Read the FDA notice itself before quoting any of this to a client.

Blinding Integrity and Psychedelic Drug Trials

This is the credibility-stress question in one title. If participants know they got the drug, the effect sizes behind MDMA and psilocybin claims need a harder look. Headline-level read only: the full text was not retrievable here, so check the paper before leaning on it. Clinical research scope, and the lens to bring to any first responder PTSD trial data.

JAMA published a piece on blinding integrity in psychedelic drug trials. Only the title reached the feed, so the argument and any data are not summarized here. The question it addresses is well known in the field: strong subjective effects tend to unblind participants and therapists, which can inflate measured benefit. Read the full text for what it recommends.

Try this: Pull the full text and note what blinding checks it asks trials to report.
