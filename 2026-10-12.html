No. 01 · Psychedelic Therapy · 2026-10-11

A living systematic review, meta-analysis, and open data resource of trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD

Primary literature on MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD, with an open data resource. The authors argue that the standard bias tools miss expectancy and functional unblinding, which is the credibility problem the field has not shaken. Treating PTSD at this acuity is clinical work, outside coaching scope, but it sets the standard of care your first responder clients will hear about.

A living systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 randomized trials, 286 participants, all sponsored by MAPS. Pooled effect on PTSD symptoms is Hedges' g of -0.71 versus control, with response RR 1.35 and remission RR 2.25. Certainty of evidence is rated low on GRADE. The authors say Cochrane risk-of-bias tools rate most trials low risk but miss functional unblinding, expectancy effects, and study conduct concerns. Short-term outcomes only.

Try this: Pull the open dataset and read the unblinding section before the next client or colleague conversation about MDMA outcomes.
Source: Neuropsychopharmacology (Nature)

Pα+ Psychedelic Bulletin #233: FDA Floats Ibogaine Trial Design; Compass Talks Pricing; Potential LSD, Psilocybin Brand Names

Regulatory weather report. FDA is circulating a psychedelic considerations document and an ibogaine trial design. Trade-press bulletin, so read it as a pointer to primary FDA documents. Ibogaine is cardiac-high-acuity; nothing here is in coaching scope.

The comment period on FDA's Considerations for Potential Future Therapeutic Use of Psychedelic Drugs closed October 5. FDA also issued a request for information sketching an early ibogaine trial: dose-escalating cohorts capped at 10 mg/kg, one participant dosed at a time, inpatient with up to 36 hours of cardiac monitoring, CYP2D6 genotyping, washout of opioid agonists, antipsychotics, antiemetics and SSRIs, and 12 months of cognitive testing. Compass pricing and brand-name items are headlines only in the page we could read.

Try this: Open the FDA documents the bulletin links and note what the framework expects of facilitator training and integration support.
Source: Psychedelic Alpha
