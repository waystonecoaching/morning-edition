A living systematic review, meta-analysis, and open data resource of trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD

Peer-reviewed, and open data, which is the right answer to a field under credibility stress. A living review lets anyone check the pooled effect as trials are added instead of trusting a sponsor's framing. Clinical-adjacent and high-acuity: this is evidence about treatment, not something that sits inside coaching scope. Read the methods for how it handles functional unblinding and the retracted or contested trial data before you quote an effect size.

Published 11 October in Neuropsychopharmacology. The authors built a living systematic review and meta-analysis of MDMA-assisted therapy trials for PTSD and released the underlying data as an open resource. The feed gives only the title and citation, so the pooled results are not summarized here. Open the paper for effect sizes and the bias assessment.

Try this: Pull the open dataset and read how the authors handle expectancy and unblinding. That is the question you will get from clients and colleagues.
