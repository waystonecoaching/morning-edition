A living systematic review and meta-analysis of MDMA-assisted therapy trials for PTSD

The most useful kind of MDMA paper right now: not a new claim, an open audit of all of them. Read it for effect sizes after functional unblinding and trial quality, not for the headline. Scope note: this is clinical PTSD treatment, outside coaching scope. Your lane is preparation and integration only.

Per the headline, the authors built a living systematic review and meta-analysis of MDMA-assisted therapy trials for PTSD, with an open data resource that updates as new trials land. I only had the headline and source from the feed, so check the full paper for pooled effect sizes, risk-of-bias ratings and how they handle unblinding.

Peer-reviewed and squarely on target: peer supporters trained in TRiM, a structured post-incident approach. This is the kind of evidence a department like yours can point to when it decides how to build or fund peer support. Scope edge: TRiM is peer screening and triage, not treatment. The study asks whether training first responder peer supporters in TRiM is feasible and acceptable. It is an EarlyView feasibility paper, so expect findings on uptake and acceptance rather than clinical outcomes. Check the abstract for sample size and which services took part.
