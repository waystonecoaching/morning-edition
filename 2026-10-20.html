How moral injury worsens burnout

Sits in the structural-critique camp: burnout read as a symptom of betrayal and values conflict, not a resilience deficit. That is the opposite pole from the HPRC-style fix-the-individual frame, and the tension is the content. Useful language for responders who hear 'take a vacation' as an insult. Scope note: where this shades into suicidality or substance use, it is clinical, not coaching.

The feed carried only the headline. The title claims moral injury drives and deepens burnout. Check whether the author cites Litz or Shay and whether any intervention is offered beyond awareness.

Try this: Ask one client this week where the job asks them to act against their values, before asking about stress.
