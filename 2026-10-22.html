The pendular regulation model: a transdiagnostic approach to emotional regulation

A new transdiagnostic theory that reaches for stress physiology and hierarchical regulation, which puts it near your autonomic frame. It is a theoretical proposal, not a tested model, and it is not polyvagal theory. Read for whether it earns its claims against Porges and Dana or just renames them.

Frontiers in Psychology, 22 October. The authors propose the Pendular Regulation Model, a neurodynamic framework meant to unify nonlinear dynamics, predictive processing, stress physiology and identity stabilization across diagnoses. They position it against HiTOP, RDoC and network models. Abstract only; no empirical test is described in the excerpt.

Source: Frontiers in Psychology
