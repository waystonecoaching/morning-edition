The impact of antidepressant use on MDMA fatalities: A matched case-control study using a post-mortem database

Safety data, which is the right counterweight to the hype. Antidepressants are first-line for PTSD, so many responders show up already on them. Post-mortem databases carry heavy selection bias, since they hold only people who died, often on street MDMA. Do not carry the result across to clinical dosing. Scope edge: medications and fatality risk are medical territory. Referral and a clear line, not advice.

A matched case-control study in the Journal of Psychopharmacology uses a post-mortem database to ask whether antidepressant use changes the odds of MDMA-related death. The abstract opens by noting MDMA is under study for PTSD and that antidepressants are the first-line drug treatment. Findings and effect sizes are unread.

Try this: When a client mentions a psychedelic plan, ask what they take now and send the medication question to their prescriber.
Source: Journal of Psychopharmacology (SAGE)
