**The story:** Swapan Samanta proposes that kundalini experiences can be read, without metaphysical commitment, as a state-dependent expansion of perceptual capacity. **Actionable:** Hold the integration-capacity idea loosely as a question to put to clients in distress after intense practice, and refer anything that looks like psychosis.
