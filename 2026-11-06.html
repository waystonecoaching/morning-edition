Compass Says NDA Will Be Filed Within Two Weeks, Declines to Comment on Usona

Compass is a for-profit company talking to investors, and the claims about speed come from management. The indication is treatment-resistant depression, not PTSD. Still, the priority voucher and a first-half 2027 launch target say how fast this could move, and that raises the stakes for how responder programs prepare.

Compass Pathways told investors on its Q3 call that the final NDA for COMP360 psilocybin in treatment-resistant depression is nearly done and will be filed within two weeks. CEO Kabir Nath tied the filing to a possible quick approval and a launch targeted for the first half of 2027, and said the PTSD program is moving. Management says a priority voucher could cut review to one or two months. No new trial data came with the update, and the company declined to comment on Usona, which is racing it toward a major depression label.

Try this: Note the date of filing when it lands. Until then it is guidance, not a fact.
Source: Psychedelic Alpha

MAPS Responds to Report of Progress for MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD with FDA

This is the MDMA story under credibility stress, and it is August, not this week. Read it for what the resubmission leaves out. No new Phase 3, and the FDA's 2024 concerns about durability, safety characterization and bias sit unanswered in the public record I can see. MAPS is also putting distance between itself and the sponsor. This sits on a clinical edge for you: PTSD, high acuity, responder clients who may ask.

Psychedelic Alpha reported on 9 August that Resilient Pharmaceuticals, formerly MAPS PBC and then Lykos, resubmitted its MDMA-assisted therapy application for PTSD. The FDA rejected the earlier application in 2024 and asked for more on how long effects last, better safety characterization, and steps to reduce bias. MAPS responded that it started and funded the underlying research but has no active role in Resilient's development. Founder Rick Doblin noted the resubmission has no new Phase 3 study. MAPS also pointed to the FDA's July 2026 final guidance on psychedelic clinical investigations.

Try this: If a responder asks about MDMA for PTSD, the honest answer is that it is under review and unapproved, and that the open questions are durability, safety and blinding.
Source: MAPS
