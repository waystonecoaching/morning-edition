A living systematic review, meta-analysis, and open data resource of trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD

Primary literature, which is what this field needs more of. A living review with open data lets anyone check the pooled effect instead of trusting a press release, and that matters while MDMA trial credibility is still bruised. PTSD with comorbid substance use and suicidality is clinical territory, well outside coaching scope. Know the evidence so you can say plainly what it does and does not show.

Published 11 October in Neuropsychopharmacology: a living systematic review and meta-analysis of trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD, released with an open data resource. The publisher blocked the full text, so I read the title and metadata only. I have no effect sizes, trial counts, or notes on blinding and bias to report. Treat this as a pointer to read in full.

Try this: Pull the full text and check the pooled effect, the handling of functional unblinding, and whether any trials enrolled responders.

No. 06 · Psychedelic Therapy · 2026-10-22
2.1Mveterans in VA care with PTSD, depression, or treatment-resistant depression

Ten VA Sites, REMS and Ibogaine's "Uphill Battle": Feds Weigh In on Psychedelic Access

This is what access will look like in practice: few sites, few patients, safety first. It is a veteran system, not a responder one, so no direct line to Phoenix Fire. It does say what the first year of supply will be, and that bears on any client asking when they can get it. Treatment itself stays clinical and off your table.

At the Psychedelic Healthcare Forum, VA senior advisor Andrew Gradison said the VA has picked ten sites for initial psychedelic treatment, chosen for prior trial experience, and would not name them. About 2.1 million of the 6.4 million veterans using VA care have PTSD, major depression, or treatment-resistant depression. If drugs reach market in January or February, the VA would start treating right away, but he expects demand to exceed supply and called treating four digits of patients in year one a challenge. The VA plans to collect real-world evidence. The excerpt I read cut off before the REMS and ibogaine sections.

Try this: If a client asks, tell them access in year one is measured in thousands, at a handful of sites.
Source: Psychedelic Alpha

2026 Third Quarter: Legislative Wrap Up

At least 23 states introduced psychedelic legislation in 2026. Five states enacted trigger laws to streamline psilocybin access after an FDA approval, and at least ten support ibogaine research. Louisiana SB 43 took effect 1 August and creates a psychedelic therapy research initiative funded partly by opioid settlement money. California's AB 113 appropriated 4.7 million dollars for psychedelic research aimed at lowering barriers for veterans. The FDA held a hybrid public hearing on 14 September on REMS, credentialing, extended monitoring, and using state program data as evidence.

The FDA hearing centered on REMS, provider credentialing, and extended patient monitoring.

MAPS is an advocacy body, so the framing runs optimistic. The facts underneath are checkable. Note what is absent: no first responder provisions and no Arizona.

Try this: Watch the credentialing debate. That is where a coach with integration training would find out what scope holds.
