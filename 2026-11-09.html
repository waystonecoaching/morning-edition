Primary literature on the claim the whole field leans on. A living review means the pooled effect gets updated as trials land, including the ones after the credibility fights over the Lykos data. Published 11 October in Neuropsychopharmacology. It pools trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD and ships an open data resource others can rerun. I have the title and abstract line only, not the full paper, so check the bias and blinding sections before you quote any number.
