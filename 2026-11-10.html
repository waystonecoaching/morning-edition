A living systematic review, meta-analysis, and open data resource of trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD

Primary literature, high credibility, and the right kind of object: a living review with open data means the MDMA/PTSD evidence base can be re-cut as trials land, not argued from press releases. Read it as the field's attempt to rebuild credibility after the Lykos fallout. Headline-level read only; I have not seen the effect sizes or the bias assessment, so check how it handles functional unblinding and therapist-adjunct effects before you cite it.

Neuropsychopharmacology published a living systematic review and meta-analysis of MDMA-assisted therapy trials for PTSD on 11 October 2026, paired with an open data resource. The feed gives the title only, so pooled effect sizes are not confirmed here.

Try this: Open the paper and pull the risk-of-bias section and the responder or veteran subgroup data before you say anything about it to a client.
