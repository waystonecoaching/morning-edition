### 01. A Living Meta-Analysis of MDMA-Assisted Therapy for PTSD, With the Data Left Open

**Source:** Neuropsychopharmacology (Nature)  \
**Category:** psychedelic  \
**Published:** 2026-10-11  \
**Link:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-026-02575-z](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-026-02575-z)

**Why this matters to you:** The field has been running on press releases and one sponsor's trials. A living review with open data is the other way to do it: pool every trial, publish the spreadsheet, update as new work comes in, and let anyone check the arithmetic. That matters more than whatever today's pooled effect size says, because the credibility problem in MDMA was never just the numbers. It was who held them. I could not get past the paywall to read the effect estimates myself, so take this as the method story until you read the paper.

**The story:** Published October 11 in Neuropsychopharmacology, the paper sets up a living systematic review and meta-analysis of MDMA-assisted therapy trials for PTSD, paired with an open data resource. A living review is built to be updated as new trials report, rather than frozen at one date. The open data piece means the trial-level inputs are available for others to reanalyze. Earlier reviews in this space have split on how much functional unblinding and therapist allegiance inflate the effects; this format makes those questions checkable instead of arguable.

**Actionable:** Bookmark the open data resource. Next time a client or chief cites an MDMA number, check it against the pooled set before you repeat it.
