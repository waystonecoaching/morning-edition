**Why this matters to you:** This is the structural lineage of moral injury, Shay's ground: the wound is what was done to you by the people above you. If betrayal drives the drinking harder than PTSD does, then a PTSD-only frame misses the man in front of you. The sample is veterans, not firefighters, but the shift-culture parallel is plain enough. **The story:** A Psychological Medicine paper from VA, UCSF, and Yale researchers drew on 1,321 combat-exposed veterans in the National Health and Resilience in Veterans Study. Witnessing tracked with drug use disorder. It is a single cross-sectional snapshot with modest odds ratios, so it cannot say which came first.

**Actionable:** Add one plain betrayal question to intake: who above you broke faith, and what did you do with it?
